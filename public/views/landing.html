Isolation you can verify

The core guarantee of this platform, explained plainly.

🧱

Every row is tenant-scoped

Companies, users, invoices, usage and every other record carry an explicit tenant id. Application code always filters by it — there is no query path that can cross the boundary.

🔑

Server decides your tenant, not you

Your tenant is bound to your session the moment you log in. The browser never sends a tenant id, so there's nothing a client could tamper with to reach another company's data.

🛡️

Hardened by default

Session fixation protection, CSRF-guarded writes, hashed passwords, and prepared statements on every query — the basics done correctly, everywhere.

Frequently asked questions

Is my company's data really isolated from other tenants?

Yes. Every tenant-owned table carries a tenant id, and every application query is scoped to the tenant bound to your logged-in session — never one supplied by the browser. One company cannot address, let alone read, another's rows.

Can I change plans later?

Anytime, from the Subscription tab in your Control Center. Switching plans updates your seats, resource limits and billing immediately — no downtime, no support ticket required.

What happens if I hit my plan's resource limit?

You'll see it coming on the usage meter before you get there. Once you hit the ceiling, further metered actions are blocked until you upgrade or the next billing period starts.

Do you offer a free plan?

Yes — Starter is free forever, with enough seats and monthly resource allowance to fully evaluate the platform before you pay for anything.

Who can access the admin console?

Only platform operators with the super_admin role. It's a separate console from your company's dashboard and cannot be reached by regular tenant users.
